Profiles

Each profile has its own cookies, logins, history, bookmarks and settings, kept only on this computer. Use one for work and one for personal, for example. Each opens in its own window.

Languages

Privacy

ProfileSets the speed-vs-privacy switches in one click: preloading, keeping sites separate, font blocking, fingerprint protection, ad blocking level and DNS. You can still change any of them below. Restart Goat to apply fully.
Fingerprint protectionStops sites from identifying your device by its hardware, GPU and browser details.
Passkeys and security keysSome sites start a passkey sign-in on their own, which makes your system pop up "use your security key".

Forget when closed

When the last tab of one of these sites closes, its cookies and saved data are deleted. You can also right-click a tab → "Forget … when closed".

      Tor

      StatusOff

      Tor replaces the proxy below while it's on. "New circuit" gives new connections a different Tor IP. Tor hides your IP, but it isn't Tor Browser: logging in to sites still identifies you.

      Proxy

      SOCKS5 sends DNS through the proxy. Chromium doesn't support SOCKS5 passwords: use an HTTP(S) proxy for login-protected servers.
